# Put SolarShield on your phone

SolarShield installs straight from your browser — **no App Store, no account, no download store.** Once it's on your home screen it opens full-screen and keeps working **offline**, which is the whole point of the app.

You just need the SolarShield web address (the hosted link at the top of [README.md](./README.md)). Open it once while you have signal so it can cache the latest data.

---

## iPhone & iPad

Use **Safari** (the install action lives in Safari's Share menu; other iOS browsers don't have it).

1. Open your SolarShield link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow going up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. Launch **SolarShield** from your home screen.

The app also shows a one-line reminder of these steps the first time you open it in Safari.

---

## Android

Use **Chrome** (or any Chromium browser — Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet).

1. Open your SolarShield link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** on the banner Chrome shows — or open the **⋮** menu and tap **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm **Install**.
4. Launch **SolarShield** from your home screen or app drawer.

*If a signed Android app (`.apk`) has been provided instead, just open that file and tap Install.*

---

## What you get

- **Full-screen app** — no browser bars, its own home-screen icon.
- **Works offline** — the last reading and the NOAA advisory text are cached on your device, so the countdown and the **Ask** tab keep answering with no signal.
- **Always sourced** — every number still carries its NOAA/NASA source and UTC time, on the phone exactly as on the desktop.

## One thing to know

Open the app online at least once so it can cache data — offline mode replays what it last saw. On iPhone, if you don't open it for a long stretch, iOS may clear that cache; just open it once more with signal and it re-caches. Nothing to reinstall.
